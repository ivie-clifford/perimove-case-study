## Technique references
Exercise technique was checked against these publishers' public pages, paraphrased and never copied: NHS, Mayo Clinic, NASM, ACE, Cleveland Clinic, MSD Manuals and Physitrack. Cues, doses, session assembly and modifications were written for PeriMove. "Referenced" means a technique source only; it does not mean clinical validation or professional review.
- NHS pages are published under the Open Government Licence with attribution; NHS photographs are not covered by it and are not used.
- Physitrack content is all rights reserved. It was only linked to as a reference during the build and is not reproduced.
- No media from NASM, ACE or Mayo Clinic is used.
- Some references match only partly (for example, a seated two-arm row reference for the supported standing row, and a single-arm row reference using a light held weight). These are noted in the library records.
- The general-information tips cite public health pages for general advice only. They establish no outcome for PeriMove itself.
